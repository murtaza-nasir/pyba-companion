Average one-week-ahead error:
Weekly revenue (gray) and the smoothed level (blue)
Weights, last 12 weeks (0 = newest)

The 143 training weeks of company revenue. The level is started at the first week's revenue and updated every week: new level = α × revenue + (1 − α) × previous level. The bars show the weight of each of the last twelve weeks in the final level: α for the newest week, multiplied by 1 − α for each week further back. The readout is the mean absolute gap between each week's revenue and the level of the week before, from the second week on.
