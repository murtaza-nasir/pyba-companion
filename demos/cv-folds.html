The 610 training customers in five folds: fitted on (blue) and validation fold (amber)
Training accuracy (blue) and CV accuracy (purple) by depth

The 610 training customers from the chapter's split, in their order in the training set, divided into the same five stratified folds as cross_val_score with cv=5 (122 customers per fold). In each round, a tree of the chosen depth is fitted on the four blue folds and scored on the amber fold. The fold accuracies are listed on the right as the rounds are stepped through, and the CV accuracy is their mean, with the standard deviation of the five. In the lower chart, the shaded band is the CV mean plus or minus one standard deviation. The test set is not used anywhere in this explorer.
