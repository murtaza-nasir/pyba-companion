A two-product version of the chapter's promo-stock problem. Cases of first aid kits (x) at $71 profit and hand soap (y) at $39, subject to 250 ft³ of promo shelf space (1.63x + 1.93y ≤ 250) and demand caps (x ≤ 90, y ≤ 120). The shaded area holds every plan the constraints allow. Raise the target profit and the purple line sweeps across the region; the last feasible point it touches, always a corner, is the optimum.
