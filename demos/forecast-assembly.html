Each forecast is the level at the last training week, plus h weeks of trend (blue dashed line and blue circle), plus the seasonal value for that week of the year (red arrow and red label). The sum is the forecast (purple dot). The shaded band is the forecast plus the middle 95% of the one-week-ahead errors on the training weeks, from −$37,449 to +$43,645. The actual revenue of a holdout week (round dot) is shown once you move the slider to that week. The lower panel is ordered by week of the year, not by date. The thirteen seasonal values used for the holdout are drawn in red, and the value for the selected week is outlined.
