The 610 training customers from the chapter's split. Each dot is one customer, in the upper band if the customer stayed (blue) and in the lower band if the customer churned (red), spread slightly so that overlapping dots stay visible. Revenue and average order value are drawn on a logarithmic axis. The weighted Gini impurity is the average of the two groups' impurities, each weighted by its number of customers. The best split for the chosen feature, with the lowest weighted impurity, is marked with a dot on the lower chart.
