The 610 training customers from the chapter's split, by weeks since their last order at the cutoff. Churners are drawn at probability 1 (red) and customers who stayed at 0 (blue), spread slightly so that overlapping dots stay visible. The score on the left is a straight line and can take any value. The logistic function on the right converts every score into a probability between 0 and 1. The number below the charts is a geometric average over the training customers of the probability the curve gives to what actually happened (churn for a churner, stay for a customer who stayed). Fitting a logistic regression means choosing the intercept and slope that make this number as large as possible.
